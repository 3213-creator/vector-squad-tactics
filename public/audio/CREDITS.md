# Bundled game sound effects

## Gunfire (rifle.wav / sniper.wav / pistol.wav)
- "Gunshot Sounds", uploaded by Tabasco; original archive copyright (c) 2009 Vincent Sevedge.
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
- Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/sounds.zip
- The page lists CC0, but the archive's creativecommons.txt specifies **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**. We retain attribution and use the stricter archive license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Changes: extracted individual shots from SKS, Mosin Nagant and CZ recordings; trimmed, faded and converted to mono 22.05 kHz WAV for positional game audio.

## Footsteps and grenade handling (step1.wav / step2.wav / throw.wav)
- "Footsteps Leather, Cloth, Armor" by HaelDB.
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-leather-cloth-armor
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Changes: leather footsteps converted to mono 22.05 kHz WAV; one metal footstep trimmed and filtered for grenade handling.

## Grenade explosion (explosion.wav)
- "Synthesized explosion" by qubodup (Iwan Gabovitch).
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/synthesized-explosion
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Changes: converted from FLAC to mono 22.05 kHz WAV with a fade-out.

## Background music (user-provided recordings)
- `main-theme-wot.mp3`: supplied as "Main Theme - WoT.mp3". Loops on the title screen and pre-lobby menus.
- `lobby-theme-mw2.mp3`: supplied as "Call of Duty Modern Warfare 2 - Main Theme (Official OST) - Dan Allen Gaming.mp3". Loops in the room lobby.
- `result-victory.mp3`: supplied as "World of Tanks Blitz - Victory Theme - Hawk1000.mp3". Plays once for the winner on the final results screen.
- `result-defeat.mp3`: supplied as "World of Tanks Blitz - Defeat Theme - Hawk1000.mp3". Plays once for the defeated player on the final results screen.
- `battle-intro-abbey.mp3`: supplied as "World of Tanks - Official Soundtrack Abbey - Wargaming Europe.mp3". Uses the first 10 seconds, encoded as stereo 160 kbps MP3 with a smooth three-second fade-out; plays once from the start of match preparation.
- Other music files are unchanged; browser playback uses reduced volume. Confirming the results switches back to lobby music.
- Countdown hammer/steel impacts are synthesized in the browser with inharmonic resonances, a short noise strike and a low hammer thud.
